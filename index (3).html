Inspiration

Everything in this store starts with a love of art in motion and a feeling for beauty in small, ordinary things.

Art in Motion

Animation taught me that a still image can feel alive. Hand-drawn frames, gentle movement and the way a character breathes between moments all shape how I think about colour, flow and rhythm in my pieces.

Colour and Light

I'm drawn to soft glows, dreamy skies and painted backdrops where every colour feels chosen on purpose. Those palettes show up in my paintings, glazes and jewelry finishes.

Everyday Beauty

A well-made mug, a patterned vase, a small pendant: beautiful objects make ordinary days feel a bit more special, and that is what I want my work to do.

About

Hello, I'm KD

I am an art creator, a lover of art, an artisan and so many more, my love for art is undefinable.
I used to think art was just a picture, nothing more, nothing less, but it's different, Art is different. There are so many things, values you can find in an art if you just calm your mind and listen to it.
I am based in the UK, although it's nothing much, I love creating artpieces, and it's been my source of joy nowadays.

My work is not as popular as others, but I make pieces on creativity, it's the same as others but different at the same time.
